📚 ข้อมูลอ้างอิงและแนวทางเวชปฏิบัติ (Clinical Guidelines & Evidence) QSMI / Thai Red Cross Society / WHO SEARO 2024
  • Queen Saovabha Memorial Institute (QSMI) / Thai Red Cross: Guidelines for Management of Snakebite Patients in Thailand (2024 revision).
  • Hematotoxin Indications: Active systemic bleeding, VCT > 20 min or 20WBCT uncoagulated, Platelet < 50,000/mm³, or INR > 1.2. Initial dose: 3-5 vials in NSS 100 mL IV drip in 30-60 min. Repeat 20WBCT q6h.
  • Neurotoxin Indications: Ptosis, ophthalmoplegia, bulbar palsy, or confirmed Malayan/Banded Krait bite (give antivenom immediately before respiratory paralysis). Initial dose: 5-10 vials (10 vials for Polyvalent) in NSS 100 mL IV drip in 30-60 min.
  • Anaphylaxis Prophylaxis: Routine pre-medication not recommended; have Adrenaline 1:1000 and resuscitation equipment ready at bedside.
