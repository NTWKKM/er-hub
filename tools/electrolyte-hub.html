⚠️ 5 Critical Hazards of IV Sodium Bicarbonate
1. Paradoxical CNS Acidosis: CO₂ diffuses across BBB faster than HCO₃⁻. Worsens intracellular acidosis if hypoventilating.
2. Acute Hypokalemia: Alkalinization shifts K⁺ rapidly into cells, precipitating arrhythmias.
3. Ionized Hypocalcemia: Alkalemia increases albumin-binding, dropping iCa²⁺ and myocardial contractility.
4. Hyperosmolar Overload: 8.4% NaHCO₃ is 2,000 mOsm/L; high sodium load causes pulmonary edema.
5. Left Oxyhemoglobin Shift: Decreases P50, impairing tissue oxygen unloading.
